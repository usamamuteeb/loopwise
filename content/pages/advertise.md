{{site.name}} readers are developers, technical founders and operations people who are deciding which AI and automation tools to use. If your product helps them, we'd like to hear from you.

## What's available

- **Newsletter sponsorship.** A short, clearly labelled placement in the weekly workflow email.
- **Sponsored tool profile.** A profile page that follows our standard format: what the tool is for, who it suits and who should skip it. You provide accurate facts; we write the page and keep our usual "skip it if" section.
- **Display placements.** Standard ad units on article pages, sold through advertising networks.

## Our standards

- Every sponsored placement is labelled as sponsored.
- Sponsorship buys visibility, not a positive review.
- We decline products that aren't relevant to our readers.
