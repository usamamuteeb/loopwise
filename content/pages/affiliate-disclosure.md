{{site.name}} is free to read. It may earn money from advertising and, in future, from affiliate links. This page explains exactly how that works.

## Affiliate links

An affiliate link is a link to a tool or service where, if you click through and later sign up or buy, the company may pay us a commission. **It costs you nothing extra**, and in some cases a link may give you a discount or longer trial.

{{affiliate.status}}

Affiliate links on this site go through addresses that begin with `/go/`, and they are marked in the page code as sponsored. Articles and tool profiles that contain affiliate links show a notice near the top. Links without that notice are plain links.

## What affiliate links do not do

- They don't change the price you pay.
- They don't decide which tools we write about. We cover tools because readers need to choose between them.
- They don't exempt a tool from criticism. If a tool is a poor fit for a common job, the profile says so.

## Advertising

We display ads from Google AdSense to readers who accept cookies. We don't choose the individual ads, and an ad on this site is not an endorsement of the advertiser. You can manage ad personalisation in your [Google ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and you can change your cookie choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

## Sponsored content

If we ever publish sponsored content, it is clearly labelled as sponsored at the top. Sponsorship buys a placement; it does not buy a positive review.
