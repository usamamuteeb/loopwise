The best way to reach us is by email: [{{site.email}}](mailto:{{site.email}}).

## What to write to us about

- **Corrections.** Found an error in an article or a broken code sample? Tell us which article and which section, and what you saw. We fix factual errors quickly.
- **Topic requests.** Describe the task you'd like to automate or the tools you can't decide between. Real problems make the best articles.
- **Partnerships and sponsorship.** See the [Advertise](/advertise/) page first, then email us.
- **Press and speaking.** Include the outlet or event, the date and what you need.

## What to expect

We aim to reply within a week. We can't give personal consulting or debug private code over email, but we'll often point you to the right article or docs page.

## Newsletter and privacy requests

To stop receiving the newsletter, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any issue. To ask us to access or delete data we hold about you, email us from the address in question. More detail is in the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).
